Band selection is a crucial technique for reducing dimensionality in hyperspectral images (HSIs) while preserving discriminative spectral information. This paper proposes an evolutionary multitasking approach with adaptive cross-dataset knowledge transfer for band selection of HSI classification. The proposed method addresses the challenge of utilizing knowledge from multiple HSI datasets to improve band selection performance across different data domains.
