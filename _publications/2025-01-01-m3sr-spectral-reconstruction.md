Spectral reconstruction aims to recover hyperspectral images from RGB inputs, enabling rich spectral information capture for various applications. This paper proposes M3SR, a Multi-Scale Multi-Perceptual Mamba model for efficient spectral reconstruction. The method leverages state-space modeling with multi-scale architecture and perceptual loss to achieve high-quality reconstruction efficiently.
