Band selection plays a vital role in hyperspectral image (HSI) classification by reducing redundancy and computational burden while maintaining classification accuracy. This paper presents a multiobjective band selection approach using an adaptive particle swarm optimizer (APSO) specifically designed for remote sensing HSI data. The proposed method balances multiple objectives to identify optimal band subsets that maximize classification performance.
